# Sahara: Your Health Companion

Sahara is an AI-powered health guidance agent built for people who live far from doctors and hospitals, such as villagers, elderly people, and anyone not comfortable with complex technology.

## What it does
- Understands symptoms written in simple Hindi, Hinglish or English
- Asks one short question at a time (age, then earlier medical problems)
- Shows a clear urgency level: LOW, MODERATE or HIGH
- Suggests simple home-care steps
- Tells you which type of doctor to see and when
- Gives emergency instructions (call 108 or 112) for serious symptoms such as chest pain or breathing trouble
- Shows nearby hospitals with contact details when urgency is HIGH

## Why Sahara
Search engines and general chatbots give long, unstructured answers. Sahara gives one short reply with a clear next step. Its safety checks (emergency keywords, doctor matching, hospital list) run in code, so important information is not left to the AI alone.

## Safety
Sahara does not diagnose diseases or prescribe medicines. It gives preliminary guidance only and always recommends professional medical care when needed. It is not a replacement for a doctor.

## Built with
Python, uAgents, Fetch.ai Agentverse, ASI:One (Chat Protocol)

## Future scope
Voice input, more Indian regional languages, telemedicine and local health centre integration, government health programmes, and live nearby-hospital search.
